ABOUT THE EVENT

Market Kshetra 2026

Market Kshetra is a student-led business exhibition organized by CPE Junior College. The event provides an exciting platform where students showcase their entrepreneurial skills by setting up creative stalls, presenting innovative ideas, and engaging with visitors through unique products, services, and experiences.

From delicious food and exciting games to handmade crafts and business concepts, every stall reflects creativity, teamwork, and practical learning while making entrepreneurship enjoyable for everyone.
